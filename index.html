Welcome

I am a Research and Teaching Fellow at the Department of Government at the University of Vienna. Before joining Vienna, I was a Research Fellow at the Cluster of Excellence “The Politics of Inequality” at the University of Konstanz.

In my dissertation, I study the role of affluent individuals and their lobbies in redistributive politics and, more broadly, the interplay between economic and political inequality: the concentration of income and wealth at the top of the distribution, the role of taxation as an instrument of redistribution, and the conditions that foster more inclusive democratic participation and representation in these policy areas. Methodologically I work with survey and field experiments and with automated and manual text analysis, including recent advances in LLM applications.

I am always glad to hear from people working on related questions — feel free to get in touch.
